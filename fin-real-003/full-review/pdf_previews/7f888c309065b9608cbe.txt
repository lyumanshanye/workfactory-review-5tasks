 1.1 1 Purpose and background
 The Professional Development Conference is the firm's annual off-site program for engineering and
 shared-services staff. It began in 2014 as a one-day Denver seminar and has grown into a three-day program
 drawing attendees from all thirteen regional offices. The conference combines technical short courses,
 leadership sessions and an early career program, and since 2022 it has carried a pre-conference intensive for a
 small enrolled group.
 Last year's program in Fort Collins hosted 24 attendees; feedback asked for a venue that would let the off-site
 groups work together for the full week rather than travel in daily. This year the program moves to a mountain
 venue with the full week available, including the weekend intensive.
 The department submits a budget application to the review committee each January. This brief is the planning
 record behind that application: the event, its venue and lodging arrangements, the guest and companion
 program, the fee schedule, and the policy provisions that govern conference travel.

 1.2 2 Event overview
 The 2026 conference is hosted in Steamboat Springs, Colorado (Routt County), a town of about 13,000 in the
 Yampa valley, roughly three hours' drive northwest of Denver. Attendees fly into Hayden (HDN) or Denver
 International and reach the town by shuttle or rental car.
 The program runs from Tuesday 2 June 2026 to Friday 5 June 2026. A pre-conference intensive runs on
 the weekend before the program, Saturday 30 and Sunday 31 May 2026; enrollment in the intensive is limited
 to twelve and is set by nomination.

 1.3 3 Venue and lodging
 The host venue is the Cascade Ridge Hotel on the edge of the town, which holds the plenary room, the four
 break-out rooms and the dinner hall under one roof.
 The firm holds a contracted conference-block room rate of $219 per night plus tax at the host hotel, for
 single or double occupancy. The block covers the nights of 1 to 5 June 2026, and a limited number of rooms
 are held for the pre-conference intensive weekend for early-arriving attendees. Block reservations are made
 through the Learning & Development coordinator by the block cutoff date of 1 May 2026; after that date
 unclaimed rooms return to the hotel's general inventory.
 Attendees who prefer other lodging may arrange it themselves and submit it through the ordinary expense
 process; the town has condominium rentals, lodges and smaller inns beyond walking distance of the venue.
 Attendees arranging their own lodging are responsible for their own bookings and cancellations.

 1.5 5 Guest and companion program
 The conference welcomes guests and companions. The program offers three guest items:
  • Guest registration, which covers a guest badge, entry to the plenary and exhibit hall, and the Wednesday
    reception.
  • Guest dinner, the conference dinner on Wednesday 3 June, held in the hotel's dinner hall with a fixed
    menu.
  • Companion lodging add-on, arranged through the coordinator, which adds a companion to the attendee's
    room booking for the conference nights.

 Guests and companions are welcome at the evening receptions, and the town's summer activities schedule is
 circulated with the joining instructions. Guest tickets for the dinner and the companion arrangements are
 ordered at the time the nominee confirms attendance; the coordinator records them on the nomination form.
 Attendees' own registration is part of the conference organization and is met from the departmental program
 budget; it is not charged to the attendee or to office travel budgets.

 1.7 7 Governing policy provisions
 Conference travel is governed by section 7 of the firm's Travel & Expense Policy Manual (Conference Travel),
 in force from 1 October 2025. The provisions relevant to this program are extracted here:
  • Conference travel at company expense requires the traveler's manager's written approval, on file at the time
    the budget application is submitted.
  • An employee who has attended an externally hosted conference of two or more days within the twelve
    months preceding the first day of the event is not eligible to attend at company expense, and an employee
    still serving the probationary period may attend only with a director-level exception recorded in the approval
    file.
  • Lodging is reimbursed only up to the applicable per diem rate, and company-supported conference
    arrangements are governed by the federal conference planning framework adopted by this policy (41 CFR
    part 301-74).

 The policy incorporates the per diem provisions of the Federal Travel Regulation (41 CFR part 301-11). Among
 the quoted provisions:
  • "Your TDY location determines your maximum per diem reimbursement rate." (§ 301-11.7)
  • "When travel is more than 12 hours and overnight lodging is required you are reimbursed your actual
    lodging cost not to exceed the maximum lodging rate for the TDY location or stopover point." (§ 301-11.100)
  • "For travel of 24 hours or more, your M&IE allowance is 75 percent of the applicable M&IE rate on the day of
    departure, 100 percent of the applicable M&IE rate on full days of travel, and 75 percent of the applicable
    M&IE rate on the last day of travel." (§ 301-11.101)

 The applicable per diem tables are those published for the fiscal year of travel; the rates in force for this
 program are the FY2026 tables, effective 1 October 2025. The coordinator keeps the current table for the
 conference state with the program file.

 1.8 8 Nominations and approvals
 Nominations opened on 17 November 2025 and closed on 19 December 2025. Managers nominated staff on
 the conference nomination form, choosing a session track and recording the nominee's travel and lodging
 request; the form collects the nominee's contact email, home office, planned arrival and departure, and any
 guest or companion items.
 Each nomination carries the manager's approval capture as the coordinator recorded it at the close of the
 window, together with the approval date and any approval notes. Where an approval action happened outside
 the form, the coordinator attached the record to the nomination.
 The nomination export, the employee roster and the staff travel log accompany this brief as the program file.
 Nominations are reconciled against the roster before any determination is drafted.
