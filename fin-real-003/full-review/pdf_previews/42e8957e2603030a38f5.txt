 3.1 2871 Grand Concourse (candidate GC-2871)
 Maria Vasquez, Apt 4A. Ms. Vasquez has lived in the building since 2019 and retired from
 home health aide work last year. She described the front entrance door as her main worry: the
 electronic lock releases the door only when the interlock is powered, and during the outages in
 January and February tenants could not get the door to latch at all. She keeps a chair against
 her apartment door on nights the entrance door will not close. The elevator stopped between
 floors twice in March; the second time the fire department released the cab. Lobby lighting has
 been out on the mailboxes side since the winter. [Photographs of the entrance door and the
 interlock cabinet are in the intake folder; the building's HPD inspection history was reviewed
 with her at the second interview.]
 Kenji Yamamoto, Apt 2B. Mr. Yamamoto moved in in 2021 and works night shifts at a
 warehouse in Hunts Point. He returns at four in the morning and has twice found the entrance
 door propped open with a wedge because the closer will not pull the door shut when the
 interlock is dead. His bathroom door came off its hinge in February; the building's
 superintendent re-hung it after eleven days. A radiator valve in the bedroom sticks open and
 the apartment overheats; he shuts the valve manually and opens a window. Hallway plaster
 above the second-floor landing has been crumbling since the winter, and he reports a
 persistent smell of damp on the north stairwell.
 Petra Ivanov, Apt 5C. Ms. Ivanov has lived in the building since 2017 and works as a school
 crossing guard. Her grandson visits every other weekend, and she asked at intake that a
 window guard be installed in the kitchen; the guard arrived without hardware and the
 superintendent installed it with sheet-metal screws she supplied herself. The smoke detector in
 the hallway outside 5C chirped for six weeks before the battery was changed. Stairwell lighting
 between the fourth and fifth floors flickers; she uses the flashlight on her telephone on the way
 down to the lobby.

 3.2 2323 Walton Avenue (candidate WA-2323)
 Francisco Ruiz, Apt 3A. Mr. Ruiz has lived at 2323 Walton Avenue since 2018 and works as
 a bus mechanic at the Kingsbridge depot. The elevator fails, by his count, two or three times a
 month, and he has carried groceries up five flights on each occasion. Hallway plaster on the
 third floor is broken out in patches above the cornice, and his apartment door has never had a
 viewer; he asks who is there before opening. The building's registration and posting notices in
 the lobby have not been renewed since he can remember. He is the only household from the
 building currently working with the office, though he reports that neighbors on the fourth and
 fifth floors have similar complaints. [The intake team's walkthrough confirmed the plaster, the
 missing viewer and the out-of-date lobby postings.]

 3.3 2165 Bolton Street (candidate BO-2165)
 Marcus Simmons, Apt 1A. Mr. Simmons has lived at 2165 Bolton Street since 2015 and
 works as a porter in a commercial building downtown. The compactor room sits across the
 corridor from his door, and in warm weather the odor is strong enough that he keeps his
 window open through the night. The rear-yard fence was down between March and April; the
 superintendent re-set the posts the week after the intake meeting. Basement corridor lighting
 flickers near the meter room. He described the building's management as responsive on small
 items and slow on anything that needs a contractor. [Two further Bolton Street households
 called the intake line in May with the same compactor and lighting complaints.]

 3.4 750 Bryant Avenue (candidate BR-750)
 Latoya Dawson, Apt 2C. Ms. Dawson moved into 750 Bryant Avenue in 2022 and works in
 childcare. A bathroom ceiling leak began in January during a boiler shutdown; the ceiling stain
 has spread from the light fixture to the corner of the room. The managing agent sent a plumber
 once in February, and the leak returned within two weeks. Heat was restored after the January
 shutdown but the hallway outside her apartment stays cold. She has photographed the ceiling
 on the intake team's advice and keeps a log of the dates the stain widens.
